Privacy notice

How I handle your details as a recruiter.

Who
Sourcechain LLC (Eve Miller). Contact: eve@sourcechain.io
What I hold
Professional details: your name, LinkedIn URL, job title, company, location, work history and skills from your public profile, your work email if I found one, and notes from our conversations. If you sign up at sourcechain.io/join, also what you send me, including your resume.
Where it comes from
Your public LinkedIn profile, professional data tools, referrals, or you.
Why
To tell you about relevant roles and, only with your OK, introduce you to a hiring company. Legal basis: legitimate interest (recruiting), or your consent if you signed up.
Sharing
With a hiring company or a partner recruiter only when you agree. Service providers (such as Google Workspace) store data for me, in the EU and the US.
How long
Up to 5 years after our last contact. If we've never been in touch, I keep only basic profile details after 12 months. If you opt out, I keep just enough to make sure I never contact you again.
Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to me holding it. Email eve@sourcechain.io and I'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
