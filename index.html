How it works

The agents cover the market. I spend my time with people.

◆ What the agents do

Every day, across the whole market.

  • Map the marketTarget companies and teams researched, profiles matched to the brief, and past candidates resurfaced when they fit a new role.
  • Verify before outreachEach person's current role is checked the same day, and contact details are verified. Nothing is guessed.
  • Reach out and follow upMessages written from each person's real background, with follow-ups on schedule.
  • Catch every replyEmail and LinkedIn replies are picked up and logged, and the pipeline updates itself.
  • Watch for timingLayoffs at target companies are flagged daily, so strong people hear from you early.
● What I do

Where judgment matters.

  • Intake calibrationWith the hiring manager, on what the role really needs.
  • ScreenFor motivation and readiness alongside fit.
  • ShortlistA small number of strong candidates, each with a clear reason they fit.
  • CloseThrough offer, acceptance and start date.
Wider market coverage and a faster first shortlist, without paying for a bigger team.
